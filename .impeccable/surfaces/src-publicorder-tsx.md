Scope: public order page `/pedido` (customers of Saint Cakes, Joinville). Visitor mode: operate (the customer completes an order request on a phone, usually arriving from Instagram or WhatsApp in daylight).

Job: pick products with real photos, see the running total and 50% deposit at all times, choose date and pickup/delivery (bairro fee), leave contact; the team confirms on WhatsApp. Constraints: server rules for zones and minimum notice, prices are estimates, quoted items have no price, no invented products or policies. Photos come only from the shop's own catalog; stock-looking pages (22–26) are excluded.

## Direction contract

THESIS: ordering is filling a Saint Cakes box: the box is always present, fills visibly, and is the path to checkout. Refuses the long form with number fields and the generic delivery-app list.

OWN-WORLD: chocolate #392317 header band and box bar, cream #FAF7F0 ground, gold #936326 for prices and selection, leaf green from the photos for confirmation. Display Gloock, text Onest, self-hosted. Real garden-light photos, rounded 20px photo frames, quantity steppers as pill controls.

STORY: the visitor sees the real cakes in garden light, taps + on what they want, watches the box fill with the total, opens the box, says when and how, and sends; they leave knowing the team will confirm on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: chocolate band with the badge logo and the line "Encomendas Saint Cakes"; a large catalogue photo of a cake in outdoor garden light beside the title "Monte sua caixa"; category tabs pinned below; the box bar fixed at the bottom on phones and as a right column on desktop.
